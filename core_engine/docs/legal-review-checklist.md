# Legal Review Checklist

These documents are founder drafts and should be reviewed before commercial sale, public marketplace listing, or enterprise deployment.

## Documents To Review

- `LICENSE.md`
- `TERMS.md`
- `PRIVACY.md`
- `SECURITY.md`
- `SUPPORT.md`
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`
- Marketplace terms for paid plugins.
- End-user license agreement for packaged installers.
- Third-party dependency notices.

## Business Questions

- Is the repository public source-available or private until release?
- Are contributors required to sign a CLA?
- Will plugin authors keep ownership of plugins?
- What revenue share applies to marketplace plugins?
- What refund policy applies to standalone sales?
- What support level is included with purchase?
- What enterprise terms are available?

## Compliance Questions

- Does cloud bridging implicate provider-specific terms?
- Are export controls or sanctions restrictions relevant?
- Are any security, optimization, or performance claims regulated by marketplace rules?
- Does the product process personal data in any hosted workflow?
- What third-party licenses are included in release builds?
